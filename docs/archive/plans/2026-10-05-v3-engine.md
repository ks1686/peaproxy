### T6 delivered

Routing is now quality-constrained, price-first, cache-aware, and guarded.

- **Cache warmth** comes from observed cache reads, not from assumption. No
  provider API reports cache state, so a recent read is a prior about the next
  turn and is documented as one. Warmth is a tie-breaker only: it never
  overrides a cheaper cold deployment, because a cache read costs a fraction of
  a fresh token rather than nothing. It never decides when either candidate is
  unpriced, and never runs against a pinned provider.
- **freeOnly** disqualifies anything without a verified zero price, on every
  route, and refuses loudly rather than falling back to paid.
- **spendCeilingUSD** measures before it trusts itself. When calls in the window
  carried no published price, recorded spend is a floor rather than a total and
  the ceiling refuses, because failing open there would spend money on the
  accounts PeaProxy knows least about.

Two dead-config defects were closed along the way: `freeOnly` and
`spendCeilingUSD` had both parsed and had tests for their accessors, and
neither had a consumer.

Still open in this area: cache read/write rates are known only from OpenRouter,
so warmth cannot be priced on other providers and stays a tie-breaker rather
than a costed decision. Per-provider cache pricing is T7-adjacent work.
